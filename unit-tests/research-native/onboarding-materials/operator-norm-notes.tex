\section{Operator norm notes}
The operator norm is one-Lipschitz under operator-norm perturbations:
\[\bigl|\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}-\|B\|_{\mathrm{op}}\bigr|
\leq\|A-B\|_{\mathrm{op}}.\]
Apply the triangle inequality in both directions.
