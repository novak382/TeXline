\section{Variational bound}
\begin{claim}\label{thm:bound}
The leading singular value satisfies
\[|s_1(X)-\theta|\leq\|E\|_{\mathrm{op}}.\]
\end{claim}
\begin{proof}
Apply the triangle inequality to $\|X\|_{\mathrm{op}}$.
For the lower bound, use $s_1(X)\geq|u^*Xv|$ and
$|\theta+u^*Ev|\geq\theta-\|E\|_{\mathrm{op}}$.
Combining the two inequalities proves the claim.
\end{proof}
\section{Interpretation}
Noise controls the error scale.
This deterministic step can be reused under different noise models.
