## 公开观测与重算表格 / Public observations and a recomputed table

Kristen Gorman and Palmer Station LTER collected the penguin observations in 2007–2009. The CC0 data are distributed through *palmerpenguins* by Allison Horst, Alison Hill and Kristen Gorman. The source CSV contains 344 rows; excluding missing body mass or flipper length leaves 342 observations. Species-specific counts and arithmetic means are recalculated from that file; mass is converted from g to kg. The editing operation rounds the Adelie mean from 3.700662251655629 kg to 3.701 kg. It does not change a measured observation. Horizontal grouping rules and other values remain intact.

- [数据、署名和 CC0 / Data, credit and CC0](https://github.com/allisonhorst/palmerpenguins)
- [原研究 / Original study: Gorman, Williams & Fraser (2014), PLOS ONE 9(3), e90081](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0090081)

The recreated summary is not claimed to be a table reproduced verbatim from the paper. The original study is open access under CC BY; the manuscript uses the public data and bibliographic facts, not copied article prose.
